What we do

Care that meets you where you are.

Flexible home and family care across Connecticut, tailored to each person and adjusted as needs change. Serving Sandy Hook, Newtown, Danbury, and surrounding communities. If you don't see what you need, please get in touch.

Elderly and personal care

Respectful help with bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and everyday dignity.

Dementia and Alzheimer's care

Specialized, patient support built around routine, familiarity, and safety.
